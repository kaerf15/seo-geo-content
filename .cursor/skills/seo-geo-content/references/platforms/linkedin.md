# LinkedIn Post

> B2B point-of-view post, not a company brochure.

产出格式 → [formats.md](../formats.md)（**长文 EN**）

## 约束

- **篇幅**：300–1200 words
- **标题**：≤70 characters (or strong first line as hook)
- **语气**：concise, first-person, B2B
- **忌**：motivational fluff; press-release voice
- **结构**：contrarian/insight hook → first person → mini-case → hashtags

## 标题公式

- `Unpopular opinion: {B2B claim}`
- `{Role}s: stop {X}. start {Y}.`
- `After {N} {projects}, here's what changed my mind about {topic}`

## 分发要点

- **Hook line**：first 2 lines visible before "see more" — put judgment there
- **Whitespace**：short paragraphs; one idea per line break
- **Hashtags**：3–5 relevant; end of post
- **Link**：one link in comments or end, not every paragraph

## 发布 checklist（不写进 output）

- [ ] Hook in first 2 lines
- [ ] First person throughout
- [ ] 3–5 hashtags at end
